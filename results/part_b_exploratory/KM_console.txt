PART C: MEAN REVERSION
==========================================================================
  For each field group, the NEXT change (log value at the next
  distinct state minus the log value now) is regressed on the
  CURRENT deviation (log value now minus the median of the last
  8 distinct states, this series only).
  A slope near -1 means a deviation is fully undone by the next
  state. A slope near 0 means it persists. The interval is a 95
  percent block bootstrap over whole CALENDAR DAYS, the same unit
  PROTOCOL.md section 11 uses.
